## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🚑 Frontline | AI Emergency Platform
**🏆 DeltaHacks Winner**

`React` `Node.js` `C++` `Gemini` `Presage SDK` `ElevenLabs`

A real-time emergency assessment platform that uses AI to help evaluate injuries and communicate important information to first responders.

- Uses camera-based vital sign estimation and Gemini Vision to assess injuries and provide insights within 10 seconds.
- Includes a conversational AI 911 operator with real-time voice responses.
- Automatically generates SBAR handoff reports for EMS personnel.

### 📖 Quran Memorization App

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `SQLite` `Web Speech API`

A full-stack app for practicing Quran memorization with speech feedback and progress tracking.

- Supports recitation practice, user accounts, personalized goals, and progress tracking across all 114 chapters.
- Uses Levenshtein distance and Arabic text normalization to compare spoken recitations.
- Achieved over 80% accuracy in voice input validation.
